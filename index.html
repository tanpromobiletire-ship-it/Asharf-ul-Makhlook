Purpose & safety

Free, compassionate help for humans, animals, plants, and all living things.

Our mission

Asharf-ul-Makhlook exists to encourage people to care for one another and for all living things. Community help is intended to be voluntary and free to access, with dignity and choice.

Help is not guaranteed. It depends on available support, funds and review, and may take time.

🛡️ Stay safe

  • Never pay someone claiming a fee is required to apply for help or release aid.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Be cautious of pressure to act quickly and people pretending to be staff or donors.
  • Fraud, impersonation, exploitation and harmful conduct are prohibited.
  • Use safe public updates; avoid exposing names, precise addresses or medical and family information.

🧪 Know the limits

This is a prototype. Use fictional help records. Aid requests, pledges, delivery plans and concern reports stay in this browser; they are not sent to caseworkers, donors or emergency services.

No real donations, aid dispatch or live emergency warnings are connected. The separate account service and visitor counters use a live API; they do not make these demo help records into real cases.

For immediate danger, use the appropriate local emergency or qualified help service.
