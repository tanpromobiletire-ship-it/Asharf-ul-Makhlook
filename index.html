Purpose & safeguards

Purpose

Provide a dignified, privacy-conscious way to request assistance, manage review, document delivery and communicate limited public updates.

Prototype safeguards

  • Test data is stored locally on the device.
  • Supporter view omits applicant names and private notes.
  • No real payments, aid dispatch, identity checks or live alerts.
  • Public case updates should omit names, medical details and precise locations.
  • Human assistance and language needs can be recorded.

Launch safeguards still required

Authentication, access control, encryption, audit logging, consent management, retention/deletion policy, incident response, safeguarding workflows, partner contracts, financial controls and jurisdiction-specific legal review must be implemented before a real service launch.

Six-gate green rule

Every green item must have current-version evidence for: (1) built, (2) implemented, (3) executed, (4) tested, (5) verified, and (6) positive required results. If any gate is missing, the item is yellow or red.
